# Problem NNNN: Title

> **Describe the problem, not a solution.** A good problem doc leaves room for a volunteer group, an app,
> and a change in process to all compete. If you already have a solution in mind, write it up separately
> with `templates/solution-proposal.md`.
>
> **Facts or blanks.** Every claim either cites a source (a link, a report, "I've seen this at…") or is
> marked `[to confirm]`. **Roles, not names**: describe groups of people, never a named private person.

## Summary

One sentence: who is affected, and what goes wrong for them.

## Who is affected

The group of people who live with this problem. How many, roughly, and where? Is it one town, one
kind of workplace, everywhere?

## The problem, as observed

What actually happens today, step by step, from the point of view of the people affected. Where does it
break down?

## How we know

Evidence: links, data, news, research, or first-hand experience (say whose, by role: "as a parent of a
child in…"). Mark anything unverified `[to confirm]`.

## What exists today

Services, apps, programs or workarounds that already try to solve this, and why they fall short.
**Search before you submit.** If something good already exists, the best "solution" may be pointing
people to it.

## Scale and impact

What it costs the people affected: time, money, health, safety, dignity. How often does it happen?

## Constraints

Anything a solution has to respect: cost, privacy, accessibility, language, legal rules, geography,
technology people do or don't have.

## What success looks like

How we would know the problem is solved, ideally in something measurable ("a housebound person can get
groceries within 48 hours of asking").

## Requirements

Give each one an ID so solutions can show which ones they meet.

## Out of scope

What this problem doc deliberately does not cover.

## How this could be misused or cause harm

Required. Who could exploit a solution to this, and how? Scams, data harvesting, access to vulnerable
people, surveillance, harassment. Solutions must answer this section.
