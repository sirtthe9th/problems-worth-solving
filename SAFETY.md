# Safety

This hub is built to help people. Some people will try to use it to do the opposite: scams dressed up as
volunteer programs, apps that harvest data, or a way to reach vulnerable people. These rules exist to make
that hard, and they apply to every issue, discussion, problem doc and solution.

## The rules

1. **The hub never handles money.** No donation links, fundraising pages, payment requests or crypto
   addresses, anywhere in the repository, issues or discussions. If a solution needs money, the solution
   doc says who holds it and how it's accounted for, *outside* this repository. Anyone asking you for
   money in the name of this hub is not acting for it.
2. **No personal data about private people.** No names, addresses, phone numbers, photos or stories that
   could identify a private individual. Describe groups and roles.
3. **Coordination stays public.** Nobody should ask you to move to a private message, email or chat to
   "get involved". Real coordination happens in issues and discussions, where everyone can see it.
4. **No surveillance or targeting.** Solutions must not track, rank, expose or target individuals.
5. **Solutions that involve people must say how they keep them safe.** Any solution that involves
   in-person volunteering, vulnerable adults or minors must fill in the vetting part of section 7 in its
   `SOLUTION.md`: how people are checked, who supervises, and what happens if something goes wrong. These
   are labelled `safety-review` and aren't merged until that section is complete.
6. **Solutions that collect data must say what they do with it**: what's collected, where it's stored,
   who can see it, and when it's deleted.
7. **No secrets or real personal data in code**, including in test data.

## Signs of a scam

Be wary of anyone who:

- asks for money, gift cards or payment details to join or help
- asks for your personal details, or someone else's, before you can contribute
- pushes you to move the conversation off GitHub
- claims to speak for the hub, or for an organization, without anything to show for it
- creates urgency ("we need this by tonight")

## How to report

- **A comment, issue or user breaking these rules:** use GitHub's **Report content** option on the
  comment (the `…` menu), and email the maintainer at **[treid@nsci.ca](mailto:treid@nsci.ca)**.
- **A security vulnerability in solution code:** report it privately through
  [Security → Report a vulnerability](https://github.com/sirtthe9th/problems-worth-solving/security/advisories/new).
  See [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
- **Someone in immediate danger:** contact your local emergency services first. This hub can't respond
  to emergencies.

Reports are kept confidential. Enforcement follows [GOVERNANCE.md](GOVERNANCE.md#moderation-tools) and
the [Code of Conduct](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md).
